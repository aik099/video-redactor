Regions

On the video

  • Draw a box to add a region. It starts at the playhead and lasts 5 s.
  • Drag a region to move it, its edges or corners to resize it.
  • Dragged edges stick to straight lines and to the video's edges; a thick edge is stuck. Hold ⌥ to stop it, also mid-drag.
  • Outside its time, the selected region is greyed out. Click it to jump to its start.

On the timeline

  • Drag a bar to move its time range, its ends to change start or end.
  • Ends stick to the playhead (⌥ stops it). Double-click an end to move it to the playhead.
  • Zoom with − +, ⌘+scroll or a pinch; scroll sideways to move along.

Keys

SpacePlay / pause
← →One frame (exact once the video has played a moment)
Shift+← →One second
− +Zoom the timeline
DeleteRemove the selected region
EscDeselect the region

Effects

  • The strength slider (1–10) makes blocks and blur bigger.
  • The preview is approximate. The ffmpeg command covers 4 px and 0.1 s more around each region.
ffmpeg command: click to select, copy, run in the video's folder
